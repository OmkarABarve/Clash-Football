# Clash Royale Reference

Clash Royale is a real-time 1v1 strategy game played on a vertical arena split by a river into two halves, one per player. Each player defends three towers: two smaller Princess Towers on the left and right, and a larger King Tower in the center behind them. The river can only be crossed at two bridges, one per lane, so fights tend to happen in the left and right lanes. Each player brings a deck of 8 cards and sees 4 at a time, and playing a card costs elixir, a resource that starts at 5, caps at 10, and regenerates steadily (faster in the final minute and in overtime). Cards summon troops, cast spells, or place buildings, but troops can only be deployed on your own side (with some extra room after you destroy an enemy Princess Tower). Once deployed, troops move and attack on their own, following simple rules: most walk down the nearest lane toward the closest tower and attack whatever enemy is in range, some target only buildings, and some are ranged or flying. This creates a rock-paper-scissors dynamic where you counter a push by spending less elixir than your opponent did. Towers shoot back at troops in range, and the King Tower only activates when it's attacked or when one of the Princess Towers falls. Matches last 3 minutes, and the winner is whoever destroys more towers. Destroying the King Tower is an instant win, and if the towers are tied, overtime gives a sudden-death extra period.

For your game, the main things to borrow or replace are the elixir economy (your M's), the lane and bridge structure (football pitches are open, so you'll need to decide whether to keep lanes), and the towers, which map to your goals.
